## Know whether your idea is worth building — before you build it

Kari defines the relevant potential-buyer or user group and identifies approximately 30–40 suitable prospects based on the agreed target definition. The client approves the target list before outreach begins. Kari handles the outreach and conducts the 20 interviews.

The client receives the interview transcripts, evidence analysis, a written report, recommended changes and a BUILD, CHANGE or KILL recommendation.

## Who the service is for

The service is designed for founders, corporate innovation teams, researchers, technology-transfer teams, EU-funded projects, accelerators and investors with an identifiable target group and a meaningful development, funding or commercialisation decision.

It is not a substitute for statistically representative quantitative research and is not intended to produce positive feedback for a decision already made.

## What the service includes

- Decision and target-group definition
- Approximately 30–40 suitable prospects identified for client approval
- Recruitment of 20 interview participants
- 20 independent customer interviews
- Interview transcripts
- Analysis of problems, current behaviour, demand signals and objections
- Willingness-to-change and willingness-to-pay signals
- Written report and recommended changes
- BUILD, CHANGE or KILL recommendation

Participants may be realistic potential buyers, users or both. The service does not imply that every participant is a confirmed buyer.

## Process

1. Define the commercial decision and target group.
2. Kari identifies approximately 30–40 suitable prospects and obtains client approval.
3. Kari handles the outreach and recruits 20 participants.
4. Kari conducts and transcribes 20 interviews.
5. Analyse recurring themes, disagreements, objections and demand signals.
6. Deliver the evidence and recommendation.

Twenty interviews provide focused qualitative evidence. They do not constitute statistically representative proof or guarantee future demand.

## BUILD, CHANGE or KILL

- **BUILD:** The evidence supports continued investment, subject to the identified risks and assumptions.
- **CHANGE:** The opportunity may be meaningful, but the customer, problem, value proposition, positioning or solution should be revised.
- **KILL:** The evidence does not currently justify further investment in the direction tested.
