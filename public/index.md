## Find out if customers actually want your idea before you build it

We find 20 potential customers, interview them, analyse what they tell us, and give you a clear recommendation: **BUILD, CHANGE, OR KILL**.

The service is designed for B2B and B2C products, services, software, and research-based innovations where realistic potential customers can be identified.

## How it works

1. **You tell us your idea.** You explain the product, target customer, and assumptions you want to test.
2. **We find the right people.** We identify 20 realistic potential customers in the target market.
3. **We interview them.** We discuss the problem, current alternatives, buying behaviour, and willingness to pay.
4. **We analyse the evidence.** We identify repeated patterns, objections, demand signals, and important changes.
5. **You make the decision.** You receive a BUILD, CHANGE, or KILL recommendation.

## What the validation tests

- Problem severity
- Current customer behaviour
- Willingness to change
- Willingness-to-pay signals

## Who it is for

The service is a strong fit when you can identify potential buyers, face a meaningful investment decision, and are willing to change or stop the idea if the evidence points that way.

It is not a substitute for statistically representative market research, and it is not intended to produce positive feedback for a decision that has already been made.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kinds of ideas can you test?

We focus mainly on B2B or B2C products, services, software, and research-based innovations where realistic potential customers can be clearly identified.

### Why 20 interviews?

Twenty interviews provide a focused evidence base for identifying repeated themes and important disagreements. They do not prove market demand statistically, so the recommendation also explains the limits of the evidence.

### Do you only interview people who already know us?

No. The goal is to speak with realistic potential customers, not only people already connected to your organisation.

### What do we receive?

You receive a report containing the main findings, demand signals, objections, recommended changes, and a clear BUILD, CHANGE, or KILL recommendation.
